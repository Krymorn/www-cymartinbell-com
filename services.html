What the monthly retainer covers

  • Updates when you ask. Text and photo changes, new pages, event updates, small new features. Just email me. If you need more time than your plan includes in a month, I'll tell you first and we'll agree on it before I start.
  • Hosting and backups. Your site is hosted and backed up, and I keep it secure and running.
  • Your domain stays yours. You register and pay for your own domain name (usually $10–20 a year), so it's always in your name. I'll help you set it up.
  • Paid platforms at cost. If your site needs a paid service, like a booking or donation platform with a monthly fee, that fee is added to your retainer exactly as charged. No markup. For example: $40 retainer + $10 platform fee = $50.

Why I do it this way

I've seen nonprofits pay website studios for sites that never arrived, or get charged far more than the work was worth. I started this to be the opposite of that: a fair price, written down before anything starts, no hidden fees, and someone who actually cares whether your site helps you.

Questions

Who do you work with?

Nonprofits, community groups, schools, and local small businesses. If your team is small and your budget is real, you're who I build for.

Will we be able to update the site ourselves?

Yes. Every site is built so non-technical staff can edit text, photos, and events, and I train your team at launch.

How long does a project take?

Most websites take 2-3 weeks from our first call to launch. Custom tools depend on scope; you'll get a timeline in the proposal.

Can you work with the tools we already use?

Usually, yes: donation platforms like Donorbox and Givebutter, payment and booking tools like Stripe, Square, and Calendly, plus Google Workspace. Ask about yours.

Why is there a monthly retainer?

It mostly pays for my time. Each month includes a set number of hours for whatever updates you need, so you never have to learn the technical side or wait on someone who's moved on. It also covers hosting and backups.

Are there any other fees?

Only two things, both up front: your domain name, which you pay for directly so it's always yours, and any paid platform your site needs, which I pass on at exactly what it costs. Nothing else.

What if we have a really small budget?

Tell me what you have. I can often scope a smaller first version, or set up a payment plan.

Who will I be working with?

Me. I'm Cy, and I handle every project myself, from the first call to launch. More about me.
