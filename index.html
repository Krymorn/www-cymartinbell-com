About

I'm a senior at The Victor School in Sudbury, Massachusetts. Most of my free time goes into engineering: programming, robotics, and passion projects I start just to figure out how something works. When I'm away from a screen, I'm usually tumbling or playing guitar.

I started my web design business after seeing nonprofits I know get overcharged for websites, or pay for sites that never got built. Now I build websites and custom tools for nonprofits and small businesses at a fair price, and I stay on to keep them running. Running it myself means I handle everything, from the first conversation with a client to launch and every update after.
